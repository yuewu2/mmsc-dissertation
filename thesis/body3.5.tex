\section{Incompressible Navier–Stokes Equations}
\label{sec:cylinder-nonlinear-dwr}

This final experiment extends the automated space--time framework to a nonlinear mixed problem: incompressible Navier--Stokes flow past a cylinder. It follows the tensor-product treatment of Roth et al.~\cite{non-flow}, while retaining the automated bubble recovery localisation developed in this thesis.
