\section{Incompressible Navier–Stokes Equations}
\label{sec:ns}

% This final experiment extends the automated space--time framework to a genuinely mixed problem: nonstationary incompressible Navier--Stokes flow past a cylinder. It follows the space--time DWR viewpoint of Besier and Rannacher~\cite{BesierRannacher2012} and the tensor-product treatment of Roth et al.~\cite{non-flow}, while retaining the bubble--cone residual localisation developed in this thesis. Galerkin time stepping and the assembly of the mixed variational forms are automated in Firedrake/Irksome; the latter supports discontinuous Galerkin formulations of semidiscrete variational systems and mixed differential--algebraic equations~\cite{andrews2026automatedgalerkintimestepping}.
