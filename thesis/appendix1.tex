\chapter{Use of Appendices}

There is no need to have appendices unless you want to and you should remember that, while the examiners are required to read the main body of your dissertation, they are not required to read the appendices. This means that there should be nothing in an appendix which is vital to the reading of the dissertation.

However, you may still wish to use appendices, for example to list notation, to give more details of calculations or to provide extra results.

The order of the appendices and the references is down to personal choice, since neither contribute to the page count. However, having appendices before references can make it easier for the reader to quickly find the references section which can be very useful.
